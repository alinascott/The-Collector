The goal of this lesson is to discuss the impact of western expansion and manifest destiny on Native peoples in the American Southwest. I will include excerpts from Dreaming of Sheep in Navajo Country and Misplaced Massacre. An alternate lesson plan will touch on the interconnectedness of western expansion and the Civil War.

Reading:

Primary Sources


  • One of Linked Trader Catalogs

  • Selected Acquisition records

  • One blanket/rug


Secondary Sources


  • Marsha Weisiger, Dreaming of Sheep in Navajo Country (p. 3-30)

  • Erika Bsumek. Indian-Made: Navajo Culture in the Marketplace, 1868-1940.


Exercise:

Utilizing the acquisition records, linked trader catalogs and students will be required to think deeply on biases, national identity, and systematic and environmental racism.
