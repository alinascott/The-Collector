Cultural Definitions: The Whirling Log

Alina Scott · first published 2018-04-09 on Woven into History

It is important to note the difference in cultural definitions of the swastika, especially in regards to the time period in question.

The word swastika comes from the Sanskrit svastika, which means “good fortune” or “well-being." (see "History of the Swastika")

While twentieth-century European representations of the motif have valid negative connotations, 18th and 19th-century North American usages, in a Native American context, have different meanings.
