Textiles · 2148-606

Chief Blanket

Third Phase Transitional Style. Three broad red bands separated by two broad cream bands. The red bands have brown and black parallel stripes separated by red. The natural bands have one central stripe of brown and black adjacent stripes. These bands are broken on the edges and center by concentric diamond and half-diamond patterns, which are filled with parallel yellow and black parallel stripes in the outer frame and cream and black stripes within the central frame.
