Textiles · 1654-131

Rug

Transitional style. The base color is light brown, with dark brown stripes at the short edges. Dark brown and corral lines form a lengthwise interwoven stepped zigzag pattern framing white squares. Brown and corral squares form chevron designs at either end, and brown and corral intersecting rectangles form a cross pattern in the center.
