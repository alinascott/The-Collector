Textiles · 2148-588

Woven Sampler

Two squares woven together on three sides. One side has a large diamond comprised of serrated concentric lines in black, white, and red around a black center. There are two white half-cross designs at each edge. The second side has a large central cross design comprised of concentric lines in white, black, red, and white around a black center. There are eight small black squares outlined by white at each corner of the cross; Germantown wool.

In-person photographs revealed this piece was sewn to another sampler to create a bag or pillow. See the essay Archives in Conversation
