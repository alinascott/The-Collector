Textiles · 2148-611

Rug

From the Southwest. Traditional style large concentric diamond pattern. The colors are white, orange, rose, green, and orange, with orange with narrow zigzag outline and white stripes at either short end. There are rust-colored tufts at the four corners; homespun wool; aniline dyes.
