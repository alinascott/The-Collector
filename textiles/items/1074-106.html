Textiles · 1074-106

Chief's Shoulder Blanket

Second Phase Style. Nine-part pattern with red and blue cross designs. The crosses are set within bands of blue, red and brown stripes, which are separated by five wider brown and cream stripes on either side of the central sections. Selvages are in blue. Indigo and cochineal.
