Textiles · 2148-597

Blanket

Blue and black stripes with designs in red and white. Stepped blocks set in a zigzag pattern form diamonds and triangles through the center and corners. Two red crosses appear in four outer diamond-shaped frames, and singular red crosses occur in each of the central frames; Germantown yarn on cotton warp; indigo.
