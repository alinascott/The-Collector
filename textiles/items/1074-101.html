Textiles · 1074-101

Chief Blanket

Third Phase Style. Nine-part design with red stepped diamonds in the center and half-diamonds around the edge. There are three principal blue bands with narrow red stripes divided by two sections of alternating three cream and two blue stripes. Cochineal and indigo.
