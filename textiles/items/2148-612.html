Textiles · 2148-612

Blanket

From the Southwest. Red background, perhaps a double saddle blanket, with three regularly spaced zigzag bands running the length of the textile. The zigzags are comprised of parallel bands of green, yellow and blue. Narrow bands of outer blue and inner yellow stripes form the border of the entire textile. Wool tufts at the four corners; Bayeta red, indigo green, and 3-ply Germantown yellow yarn.
