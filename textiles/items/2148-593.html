Textiles · 2148-593

Chief Blanket

Third Phase Style. Broad black and white stripes, with narrow blue stripes in the center and on the edges. The stepped blocks are set in parallel zigzag lines of red, blue, white, and blue. They frame triangles of red in the corners and sides; Germantown four-ply weft on cotton warp.
