Decoding Catalog Cards

Alina Scott · first published 2018-04-23 on Woven into History

While viewing a catalog might seem intimidating, do not be afraid. You already have all the knowledge you need to extract information from it. Most of them are organized in a way that makes them easy to read. Just in case, here are some extra deciphering tools!
