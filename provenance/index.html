Pathway · Provenance

Provenance

Williams gave the University of Texas his whole collection by a Deed of Trust and kept it during his lifetime. After it reached Austin it was split: the textiles to the Texas Memorial Museum and later the Department of Art and Art History, the photographs and lantern slides to the Harry Ransom Center.

Chain of custody

  1. 1898-04-10Frederic Allen Williams is born, son of Dr. Frederic Allen and Elizabeth Paine Williams.PR-01 Announcement of Gift p. 3 · West Newton, Massachusetts
  2. 1922Williams keeps a studio in New York from this year (later at 58 West 57th Street).HRC-4872 lecture brochure · New York
  3. 1929Williams photographed in Taos modeling "The Sioux Worshipper" sundial.HRC-5186 slide label · Taos, New Mexico
  4. 1934Slides of Taos women photographed in Bert Phillips's studio and patio; Williams's Maid of Taos and related sculptures draw on these sittings.HRC-5154 slide label; Jesse Ritner introduction · Taos, New Mexico
  5. 1939Shrine of the Americas copyrighted.HRC-4872 lecture brochure · New York
  6. 1940Studio portraits: Williams sits on Chief Blanket 2148-606 beside the composite sampler 2148-588 and The Arrowmaker.HRC-5178; HRC-5182; Archives in Conversation · New York
  7. 1940sWilliams lectures on the "Arts of the Americas" with Kodachrome lantern slides (tenth year of lectures by the time of the brochure).HRC-4850-2; HRC-4853 · New York and touring
  8. 1948Biography reprinted from "International Who's Who."HRC-4849
  9. undatedUT Board of Regents accepts a Deed of Trust conveying Williams's entire collection: sculpture, Indian textiles, baskets, pottery and artifacts, the Horse Museum, and books, photographs and lantern slides. Williams keeps possession during his lifetime.PR-01 Announcement of Gift · Austin, Texas
  10. before 1969Collection held in storage at the Balcones Research Center, where it is catalogued.PR-02 Accession record · Austin, Texas
  11. about 1966–1968Texas Memorial Museum catalog cards made for the textiles.PR-04 Catalog cards · Austin, Texas
  12. 1969-09Texas Memorial Museum accessions the collection as Acc. No. 2148 ('Received from: Storage at Balcones, Williams Collection'), accessioned by Mary Carnahan. Other items from the collection are under TMM Acc. No. 1654.PR-02 Accession record · Austin, Texas
  13. laterTextiles held by the Department of Art and Art History, photographed for the DASe Visual Resources Collection by Steve Bourget and Kimberly L. Jones; some shown on rotating display at the Blanton Museum of Art.Woven into History (2018) · Austin, Texas
  14. laterWilliams's photographs and lantern slides held by the Harry Ransom Center (Frederic Allen Williams Photograph Collection and Lantern Slide Collection).HRC finding aid · Austin, Texas
  15. 2018Alina Scott publishes Woven into History; Jesse Ritner publishes Frederic Allen Williams: A Citizen Artist with a Magic Lantern.Project history · University of Texas at Austin
  16. 2026The two archives are rejoined in this repository.This repository

What the record doesn't say yet

  • The date of the Deed of Trust and of Williams's death.
  • When and why the photographs went to the Ransom Center and the textiles did not.
  • Who wove these textiles, and through which traders they reached Williams.
  • Catalog cards exist for textiles not yet digitized: 2148-582, 2148-583, 2148-591, 2148-604, 1074-104, 1074-115, 1238-62, 1633-75, 2617-1.
