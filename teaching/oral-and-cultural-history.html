Teaching · Lesson plan

Oral & Cultural History and their Preservation

Alina Scott · 2018-03-19

Field trip to a museum/reflect on past museum experience/ online virtual museum experience. The class will discuss general reactions to museum holdings. Who was represented in the collections? Who was not present or underrepresented? Why might that be?

Readings

Linked acquisition records, Museum website, excerpts from Give Me Liberty!
Exercise

Using acquisition records, students will research and discuss the process of acquiring museum/ archival collections and the importance of preserving cultural history, trustworthy source material, and obligations to the living when writing the past.
