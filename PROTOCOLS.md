# Cultural protocols

This collection was assembled by a non-Native collector and catalogued by non-Native institutions. The 2018 site set out to "recenter the discussion of [Diné] culture and commodity production around them." This repository keeps that aim.

## Teaching note

Every page of the site carries this note in its footer:

> This collection is intended for educational use only. It may contain harmful language and images of inappropriate dress. It should be read carefully and in the context of the teaching materials. If you believe any material should not be included on this site, contact the owner.

## Items marked for community review

`photography/photographs.csv` has a `cultural_review` column. Items with a value in it:

- **Taos Pueblo people and places** (Lantern Slide Collection series III-A): portraits, the North House, rooftop views, and a hoop dance. Taos Pueblo restricts photography of its people, buildings and ceremonies today.
- **Williams in costume** (series I-F): Williams dressed as "Indian" (1945, 1946), "Eskimo" (1946) and "Mexican" (1940). These pages need interpretive framing, not just captions.
- **Unidentified Indigenous sitters** (HRC-5108, HRC-5110).

## Before public launch

1. Consult the Navajo Nation Historic Preservation Department about the textiles and their descriptions.
2. Consult Taos Pueblo about the series III-A slides.
3. Apply [Local Contexts](https://localcontexts.org) Traditional Knowledge / Biocultural labels where communities choose to.
4. Build with `python3 scripts/build_site.py --withhold` to replace flagged images with a placeholder while keeping their catalog records, until review is complete.
5. Keep a takedown route open (see RIGHTS.md).

## Language

Catalog descriptions are kept as recorded so the archival record is visible. Where that language is outdated or harmful, the site's own text adds context rather than silently replacing it. The 2018 essay "Cultural Definitions: The Whirling Log" explains the whirling log motif on sampler 2148-589.
